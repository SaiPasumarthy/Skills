# Inclusion
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/inclusion
Inclusive apps put people first — respectful communication and content/functionality everyone can access and understand. Investigate people's goals and perspectives; use empathy; examine assumptions.
Relevance to adaptive layout: support the broadest audience across devices, languages (including RTL), text sizes (Dynamic Type), and input methods — echoing the Flexibility design principle.
Platform considerations: no platform-specific layout notes. Related: Accessibility, Right to left.
