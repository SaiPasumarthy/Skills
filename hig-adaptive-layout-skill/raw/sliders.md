# Sliders
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sliders
A slider is a horizontal track with a thumb people adjust between min and max. The portion between minimum and thumb fills with color.
Best practices: customize appearance if it adds value; use familiar slider directions — minimum on the LEADING side and maximum on the TRAILING side for horizontal sliders (this reverses in RTL — see Right to left); minimum at the bottom and maximum at the top for vertical sliders.
Platform considerations: iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS. Developer: Slider (SwiftUI), UISlider (UIKit), NSSlider (AppKit).
