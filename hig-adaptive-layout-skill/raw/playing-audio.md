# Playing audio
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/playing-audio
People expect audio that automatically adjusts when the device context changes (speakers, headphones, Bluetooth, AirPlay). Respond to volume controls, the Ring/Silent switch, Control Center.
Best practices: respond to audio interruptions and route changes; respect the silent switch; support AirPlay; duck/pause appropriately (see Multitasking).
