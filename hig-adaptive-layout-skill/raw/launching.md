# Launching
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/launching
A streamlined launch helps people start immediately.
Best practices: launch instantly. If the platform requires it, provide a LAUNCH SCREEN. In iOS, iPadOS, and tvOS the system displays your launch screen the moment the app starts and quickly replaces it with your first screen — design the launch screen to resemble the first screen so the transition feels seamless. macOS, visionOS, and watchOS don't require launch screens. Avoid splash/branding screens at launch (consider them at the start of onboarding instead).
ADAPTIVE NOTE: A launch screen must adapt to every device size and orientation (use a storyboard/auto-layout launch screen, not fixed images), so it matches the first screen across all devices.
Platform considerations: iOS, iPadOS require a launch screen. Developer: launch screen storyboard.
