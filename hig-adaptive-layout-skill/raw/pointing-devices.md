# Pointing devices
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/pointing-devices
People use a pointing device (trackpad or mouse) to navigate and initiate actions. On a Mac, people typically combine a pointing device with a keyboard. On iPad and Apple Vision Pro, a pointing device is an ADDITIONAL way to interact, without replacing touch/eyes/gestures.
Best practices: be consistent with mouse/trackpad gestures; avoid redefining systemwide trackpad gestures; support the pointer (on iPad the pointer adapts its shape to the content it hovers — design controls to work with both touch and pointer).
ADAPTIVE NOTE: Because iPad supports touch AND pointer/keyboard/Apple Pencil, design layouts and hit targets that work across all input modes (see Designing for iPadOS). Pointer interactions can enable higher information density.
Platform considerations: iPadOS, macOS. Developer: UIPointerInteraction.
