# Workouts
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/workouts
Workout/fitness experiences encourage engagement and track progress. People wear Apple Watch during workouts and might carry iPhone/iPad; larger devices (iPad Pro, Mac, Apple TV) are used for live/recorded sessions.
Adaptive relevance: a workout app spanning Watch + iPhone/iPad + Apple TV must adapt its layout to each device size and viewing distance. Largely watchOS/fitness-focused.
