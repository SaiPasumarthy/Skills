# Rating indicators
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/rating-indicators
A rating indicator uses a series of horizontally arranged symbols (by default, stars) to communicate a ranking level. Doesn't display partial symbols; symbols are always the same distance apart and don't expand/shrink to fit the component's width.
Best practices: make it easy to change rankings inline; if you replace the star with a custom symbol, make its purpose clear.
Platform considerations: No additional considerations for macOS. Not supported in iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, visionOS, or watchOS — macOS-only component. Developer: NSLevelIndicator (rating style).
