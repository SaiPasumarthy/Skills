# Live-viewing apps
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/live-viewing-apps
Prioritize live content; make it distinguishable from VOD at a glance; minimize the interval between launch and playback.
Best practices: feature live content prominently; organize tabs consistently (live, then recorded, then other — see Tab bars). Primarily a tvOS-oriented pattern but applies to iOS/iPadOS streaming apps.
