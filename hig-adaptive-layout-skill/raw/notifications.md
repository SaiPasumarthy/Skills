# Notifications
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/notifications
A notification gives people timely, high-value information at a glance. Requires consent. People customize which styles they receive and delivery times.
Anatomy (varies by platform): a banner or view on a Lock Screen, Home Screen, Home View, or desktop; a badge on an app icon; an item in Notification Center. Communication notifications (calls/messages) can feature prominent contact images.
Best practices: send high-value, timely info; keep content brief; provide relevant actions.
Platform considerations: iOS, iPadOS, macOS (and others). Developer: UserNotifications framework.
