# Color wells
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color-wells
A color well lets people adjust the color of onscreen elements; it displays a color picker when tapped/clicked (system-provided or custom).
Best practices: consider the system-provided color picker for a familiar, consistent experience across iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.
Platform considerations: No additional considerations for iOS, iPadOS, or visionOS. Not supported in tvOS or watchOS. Developer: ColorPicker (SwiftUI), UIColorWell (UIKit), NSColorWell (AppKit).
