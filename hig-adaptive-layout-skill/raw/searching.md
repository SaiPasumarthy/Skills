# Searching
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/searching
People search for content on their device, within an app, and within a document. Within an app they generally expect a search field. Personalize with recent searches, suggestions, completions, corrections. Support scoping/filtering.
Adaptive relevance: search field placement adapts per platform and to window resizing/compact width (see Search fields).
Best practices: start search as people type; simplify results; let people filter.
