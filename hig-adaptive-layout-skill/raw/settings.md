# Settings
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/settings
The system-provided Settings app lets people adjust appearance, network, accounts, accessibility, language/region — and can include per-app settings. You can provide a custom settings area within your app for general settings; put task-specific options within the task itself.
Best practices: expose only settings people need; prefer sensible defaults; on iOS/iPadOS link app settings appropriately; on macOS provide a Settings window (Cmd-,).
Platform considerations: iOS/iPadOS (Settings app or in-app), macOS (Settings window).
