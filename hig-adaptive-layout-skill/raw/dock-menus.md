# Dock menus
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/dock-menus
On a Mac, people secondary-click an app's Dock icon to reveal a Dock menu (system-provided and custom items). iOS/iPadOS don't support a Dock menu — the equivalent is Home Screen quick actions.
Best practices: label items succinctly and organize logically; make custom Dock menu items available in other places too (menu bar menus or within your interface).
Platform considerations: macOS only. Developer: NSApplicationDelegate applicationDockMenu.
