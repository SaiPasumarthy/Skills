# Onboarding
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/onboarding
Onboarding helps people get a quick start; make it fast, fun, and optional. Occurs after launching completes.
Best practices: teach through interactivity; consider context-specific tips instead of a single flow; keep it skippable; don't gate the core experience behind it.
Platform considerations: all platforms; adapt the onboarding layout to each device size.
