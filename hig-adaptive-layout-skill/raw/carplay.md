# CarPlay
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/carplay
CarPlay shows app content on a car's built-in display while the driver stays focused on the road. Apps install on iPhone; icons appear on the CarPlay Home screen when connected.
KEY ADAPTIVE POINT: You DON'T design pixel layouts — you use system-defined TEMPLATES (audio, communication, navigation, fueling, etc.). Your app provides content and iOS renders it in CarPlay, automatically adapting to the wide variety of car display sizes, resolutions, aspect ratios, and input methods (touch, rotary knob, touchpad). Design for glanceability and minimal interaction.
Best practices: use the appropriate templates; minimize interaction steps; keep content glanceable.
Platform considerations: CarPlay (driven from iPhone). Developer: CarPlay framework / templates.
