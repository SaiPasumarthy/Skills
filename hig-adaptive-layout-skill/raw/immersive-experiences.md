# Immersive experiences
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/immersive-experiences
In visionOS, apps can extend beyond windows and volumes to immerse people. Choose the Shared Space or a Full Space; support different types of immersion; use passthrough.
NOTE: This is a visionOS-only topic. OUT OF SCOPE for iOS/iPadOS/macOS adaptive layout. Retained for completeness.
