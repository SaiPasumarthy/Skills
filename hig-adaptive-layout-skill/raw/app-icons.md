# App icons
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons
A unique, memorable icon expresses your app's purpose and personality. Appears on the Home Screen and throughout the system (search, notifications, settings, share sheets).
Layer design: layers give the most control over how the icon is represented (Liquid Glass). Provide layered assets; the system composites and applies effects (light/dark/tinted).
ADAPTIVE NOTE: The system renders the app icon at many sizes and in light/dark/tinted appearances — design so it remains legible across all rendered sizes and appearances.
Platform considerations: iOS, iPadOS, macOS. Developer: Icon Composer / asset catalog.
