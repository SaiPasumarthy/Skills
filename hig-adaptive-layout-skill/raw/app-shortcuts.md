# App Shortcuts
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-shortcuts
An App Shortcut gives people access to your app's key functions or content throughout the system (Siri, Spotlight, Shortcuts app, Action button, Apple Pencil squeeze). Available immediately after install.
Best practices: expose key functions; keep phrasing natural; provide a snippet where helpful.
Platform considerations: iOS, iPadOS, macOS. Developer: App Intents.
