# Camera Control
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/camera-control
The Camera Control provides direct access to your app's camera experience on iPhone 16 / 16 Pro. A light press shows an overlay extending from the device bezel; a light double-press reveals available controls; sliding adjusts a value.
Best practices: open the camera quickly; provide adjustable controls via the overlay.
ADAPTIVE NOTE: The Camera Control is a hardware feature along the device edge — account for it in the safe area / avoid placing conflicting controls near it (see Layout).
Platform considerations: iOS (supported iPhones). Developer: AVFoundation / Camera Control APIs.
