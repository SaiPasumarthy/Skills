# Alerts
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/alerts
An alert gives people critical information they need right away (problems, destructive-action warnings, purchase confirmations). Appears centered, modal, above content on all platforms.
Best practices: use alerts sparingly; avoid using an alert merely to provide information (prefer an inline indicator); keep title/message concise; limit to two buttons where possible; identify destructive buttons.
ADAPTIVE NOTE: Alerts have a system-fixed width and center themselves regardless of device/orientation — keep content short so they remain readable at that fixed size.
Developer: alert (SwiftUI), UIAlertController.Style.alert (UIKit), NSAlert (AppKit).
