# Nearby interactions
URL: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/nearby-interactions
Nearby interactions integrate the presence of people and objects nearby (Ultra Wideband). E.g. transferring audio from iPhone to HomePod mini when brought close.
Best practices: make interactions feel intuitive and natural; provide clear feedback. Minimal direct adaptive-layout relevance.
Platform considerations: UWB-capable devices. Developer: Nearby Interaction framework.
